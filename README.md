## About me

I keep large construction projects on budget. With 10+ years in engineering and 8+ years in project cost control, I currently lead cost control for two concurrent Saudi Aramco contracts with a combined value of more than SAR 900M.

My work covers budgets and cost breakdown structures, monthly cost reporting, earned value management, subcontract and invoice review, and procurement control against BOQ and budget. I also build my own dashboards and tools in Power BI and Excel Power Query to turn slow manual checks into repeatable reports.

## What I do

- **Cost control & reporting:** budgets, CBS, monthly cost reports, forecast at completion, variance analysis, cash flow
- **Earned value management:** PV, EV, AC, CPI, SPI, EAC and VAC with clear explanations of what drives them
- **Commercial review:** subcontract and charter-party review, invoice verification, change orders, FIDIC 1999 Red Book
- **Procurement control:** material requisition and PO approval against BOQ, take-off and package budgets
- **Audit & reconciliation:** GL-to-PO reconciliation, petty cash audits, risk registers and concern logs
